MI is the estimated mutual information; ε = exp(−MI). Test R² evaluates held-out collapse conditional on the selected representation. It does not measure out-of-sample performance of the entire discovery workflow. Counts, filtering and validation splits are detailed in each full result card.

Keep the numerical evidence.

Language models formulate and check the problem. Fixed scientific tools construct admissible groups, examine local mechanisms, select representations and validate the resulting collapse.

Grounded formulation

Problem descriptions, variable roles and named SI dimensions are checked against retrieved, cited knowledge records before a basis is accepted.

Mechanism-aware discovery

Surrogate gradients support regime assessment. Similarity screening and mutual-information searches then identify local informative groups.

Reviewable validation

Simplification, held-out collapse, uncertainty and exact case settings remain attached to the saved scientific state.
