# Chart style

Profile colors follow the supplied velocity-transformation comparison:
`#7b8cde`, `#e96a73`, `#eaa05d`, `#aa7bcb`, `#59bf78`, `#af805e`.
Distinct groups use circles, squares, upward triangles, diamonds, downward
triangles and pentagons, with 10 px markers and thin white outlines.

Physical-regime colors follow the supplied regime comparison: outer/rough
turbulent `#d55e00`, intermediate `#009e73`, near-wall/viscous `#0072b2`.
Shapes also differ. Cess A/B/C denote outer/intermediate/near-wall regions;
pipe A/B denote rough-turbulent/viscous dependence. Numerical labels are mapped by
median y/h or Reynolds number, independently of raw cluster-ID permutations.
Raw IDs remain in partition JSON exports.

The page and app use English copy and descriptive image labels. Numerical
figure/table references remain only in archival provenance and technical records.

The overview uses one source schematic per physical system, grouped beneath the full similarity equations. A single SVG composes the three workflow layers from original artwork. Source records are retained in `docs/assets/workflow/original-artwork-provenance.json`.

Optimization views follow the supplied four-case notebook: paired MI contours
and surfaces, Viridis colors, a red mean trajectory, a white circular start and a gold diamond endpoint.
Response/regime scatter palettes and distinct 10 px markers remain consistent.
Optimization endpoints are smaller: 8/10 px in contours, 3.5/4.5 in 3D.

MI contours use 45 filled intervals and equal coefficient units. Contours,
surface and convergence grow/wrap with the available width. One shared key sits
below the three views and clear of every axis. Controls remain visible above
the plots. Regime criteria remain visible as four threshold cards.

Validation pairs ε and MI in one compact frame. One-group, two-group,
sparsified and rationalized stages use blue, purple, green and ochre respectively.
Dimensional matrices use signed blue/neutral/ochre heatmaps with compact integer
labels; basis vectors appear as explicit power-product equations.
